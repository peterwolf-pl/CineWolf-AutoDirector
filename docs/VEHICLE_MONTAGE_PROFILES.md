# Vehicle montage profiles

Vehicle profiles enrich framing and montage planning.

## Categories

`MINECART`, `TRAIN`, `BOAT`, `HORSE`, `MOUNT`, `GROUND_VEHICLE`, `AIRCRAFT`, `ZIPLINE`/`ZIP_LINE`, `CONVOY`, `GENERIC`

## Templates

- Aircraft takeoff / turn / landing
- Train departure / arrival
- Zip-line travel

Templates suggest shot sequences but still respect visibility, collision, duration, and available generators.
